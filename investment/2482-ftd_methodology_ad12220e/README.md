## Description

The FollowThrough Day (FTD) is William O'Neil's market timing signal for confirming a new uptrend after a correction. It is the single most important signal for reentering the market after a decline. Without an FTD, no new bull market or sustained rally has ever begun (per IBD historical analysis).
